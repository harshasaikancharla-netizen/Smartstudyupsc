Here are a few things students often want to know. If you need details about a particular exam or resource, reach out and ask us directly.

Still have a question? ?
Which exams does SmartStudy Govt cover?

We focus on government-exam preparation. Coverage can vary, so message us to check whether we currently support a specific exam.

Where can I find exam alerts?

Visit our Instagram page for alerts and updates. Always confirm dates, eligibility, and instructions in the official notification.

How should I get started?

Choose your target exam, review its syllabus and timeline, then build a steady routine. You can use the focus sprint on this page to begin with one small study session.

Can I ask about study resources or guidance?

Yes. Send us a message with the exam you are preparing for and what kind of help you need. We can point you to the most relevant information we have.

How do I check course or resource fees?

Availability and fees may change. Message us on Instagram for the latest details before making a decision.
